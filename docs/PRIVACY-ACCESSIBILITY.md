# Privacy and accessibility review

Reviewed September 11, 2026 against the National Privacy Commission's published [Data Privacy Act of 2012](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/) and [implementing rules](https://privacy.gov.ph/implementing-rules-regulations-data-privacy-act-2012/). The review informed purpose limitation, minimal account fields, transparent notices, role-limited access, and correction/deletion request wording. It is not a certification or legal compliance determination.

The operator must establish its lawful processing basis, controller/contact identity, retention schedule, service-provider arrangements, incident handling, and rights-request procedures before production collection. Review deployment location and any non-Philippine users with the responsible privacy adviser. The included policies explicitly identify these unresolved operational details rather than inventing them.

Registration uses an unchecked terms/privacy acknowledgment and collects first/last name, email and password only. Optional in-app notification preferences are separate. No analytics, ads, map/video embeds, social widgets or remote font scripts are loaded. Supabase processes database records and public crop images; the chosen hosts can process technical request data. Crop processing strips image metadata. No optional-cookie banner is needed in this configuration.

Meaningful crop photos use crop-name alternatives; the text-labeled brand image is decorative to prevent duplicate announcement. Forms use labels and native inputs/selects. Native dialog handles modal focus, Escape and focus return. Icon-only buttons carry accessible names. Focus styles are visible, cards replace management tables at smaller breakpoints, charts use responsive containers, and the data tables give an alternative to visual charts. Authenticated shells have one page-level heading rather than duplicate H1s.

The current automated/browser checks are recorded in QA.md. A full screen-reader and device-matrix audit remains part of live deployment acceptance; do not claim a WCAG conformance level without that assessment.
